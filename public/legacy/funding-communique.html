Capital and business progression

Each stage converts capital into a defined de-risking result. Later capital should follow evidence from the preceding stage.

01

Deploy the controlled environment

Need: Remote infrastructure and operating controls.
Unlock: A stable environment capable of supporting independent review and bounded pilot activity.

02

Validate security and operations

Need: Independent testing, remediation, legal scoping, reliability exercises, and operating procedures.
Unlock: Third-party evidence and a defined risk register.

03

Run the controlled market pilot

Need: Merchant activation, participant onboarding, support, safeguards, and measurement.
Unlock: Adoption, engagement, transaction, dispute, retention, and operating-cost evidence.

04

Close institutional gaps

Need: Final legal analysis, sponsor-bank diligence, partner procedures, connectivity, and external approval work.
Unlock: A defensible production-readiness decision.

05

Enter the market deliberately

Need: Commercial onboarding, partnerships, customer acquisition, support capacity, and monitored rollout.
Unlock: Repeatable go-to-market evidence and initial operating economics.

06

Scale after validation

Need: Capacity, team expansion, institutional integrations, resilience, and geographic or segment expansion.
Unlock: Growth based on verified demand and completed external gates.

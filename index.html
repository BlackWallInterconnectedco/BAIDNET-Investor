Readiness Assessment

BAIDNET demonstrates strong platform readiness with clear identification of external requirements for market entry.

Strong / Established Areas

  • Governance framework
  • Auditability controls
  • Participant validation
  • Closed-loop controls
  • Sponsor-bank boundary definition
  • Customer protection framework

Areas Requiring External Closure

  • Legal classification review
  • Securities analysis
  • MSB / money-transmission review
  • Sponsor-bank due diligence and onboarding
  • Contractual responsibility allocation
  • Partner-specific production connectivity
  • Procedural formalization and applicable sign-offs

BAIDNET demonstrates strong readiness for sponsor-bank engagement. The primary remaining commercialization requirements are external legal, regulatory, contractual, sponsor-bank, and production-enablement steps rather than rebuilding the core platform.

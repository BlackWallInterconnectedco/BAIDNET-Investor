Platform Overview

BAIDNET is structured as one platform with distinct settlement paths and shared transaction architecture. The blockchain side supports digital-asset settlement; the fintech side extends the same core architecture into fiat workflows behind regulated sponsor-bank boundaries.

Sponsor-Bank Readiness

BAIDNET distinguishes between what has been completed internally and what remains dependent on sponsor-bank production validation, formal attestation, or contractual onboarding.

Internal Engineering Complete / Pass

  • Fiat transaction lifecycle engineering
  • Versioned financial primitives API
  • Risk and routing controls
  • Reconciliation and exception workflow
  • Dispute operations
  • Runtime and audit evidence surfaces
  • Workstream A-G consolidated closeout

External Dependencies Remain Open

  • Live sponsor-bank settlement verification
  • Sponsor-bank callback parity verification
  • Sponsor-bank reconciliation parity verification
  • Partner-specific ACH / debit / FBO onboarding
  • Completed sponsor-bank attestations and sign-off
Readiness language: Internal engineering completion does not mean bank approval, regulatory approval, commercial launch, or production banking live. BAIDNET's internal controls and integration boundaries are engineered and evidence-backed; remaining live sponsor-bank gates are explicitly external.

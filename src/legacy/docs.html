00 · Two-Minute Executive Brief

BAIDNET at a glance.

The shortest path from the investment thesis to the evidence boundary.

What it is

Integrated financial & community-commerce infrastructure

BAIDNET combines blockchain-native capabilities with a separately governed traditional-currency FinTech path inside a unified product experience.

What exists

Implemented platform capabilities

Evidence presented across the project covers wallets/accounts, merchant and payment workflows, blockchain execution, FinTech transaction primitives, APIs, auditability and deployment artifacts.

What is verified

Internal engineering scope

Documented workstreams substantiate transaction controls, routing boundaries, idempotency, callbacks, correlation, reconciliation, audit trails, runtime checks and regression safety within their stated scope.

What remains

External activation gates

Legal classification, sponsor-bank diligence and approval, partner-specific compliance configuration, live production settlement validation, independent security review and applicable authorization remain external.

Current business phase

Controlled-pilot preparation

The immediate objective is to convert verified engineering into secure remote operating evidence, independent validation and measurable controlled-pilot results.

What capital changes

Evidence becomes operating proof

Capital funds the environment, independent review, legal scoping and pilot preparation needed to move from internal engineering evidence toward institutional diligence and market evidence.

03 · Transaction Model

Dual-rail financial architecture.

Blockchain rail

BAIDNET-owned ledger activity follows blockchain transaction controls, signatures, nonce/replay protections, consensus/finality behavior, receipts and ledger evidence. Supported blockchain interactions remain distinct from USD fiat activity.

Fiat / FinTech rail

The FinTech path uses a separately governed transaction lifecycle designed for sponsor-bank connectivity, including idempotency, callbacks, correlation, reconciliation, audit evidence and partner-specific production controls.

04 · Core Technology

Blockchain · QUAD VM · BAIDPAY · FinTech.

BAIDNET Blockchain

Native ledger and transaction environment with validator/consensus behavior, persistent ledger state, transaction signatures, finality-oriented payment flows, monitoring and operational runtime components.

QUAD VM

Execution environment and language/runtime workstream spanning grammar, IR, interpreter execution, settlement integration, programs/storage/registry/state-root behavior and later PBFT/persistent-node/runtime work.

BAIDPAY

Payment experience covering wallet/account interfaces, peer-to-peer and merchant flows, payment requests, receipts, history, claims/refunds and blockchain-supported payment interactions.

FinTech Extension

Traditional-currency transaction primitives around a controlled lifecycle, with routing, idempotency, callback ingress, event persistence, correlation, audit trails, reconciliation, runtime governance and regression safety.

05 · Evidence Center

Proof is separated from readiness.

Product proof asks whether the technology and workflow exist. Readiness evidence asks what has been verified, under what scope, and what still gates deployment.

Product proof

Customer/business interfaces, wallet and merchant flows, transaction lifecycle behavior, claims/refunds, governance/reporting functions, mobile access, APIs, blockchain execution and deployment artifacts.

Readiness evidence

Workstream verification, routing boundaries, callback/replay controls, idempotency, correlation, reconciliation, audit-trail reconstruction, runtime checks, regression tests and documented external dependencies.

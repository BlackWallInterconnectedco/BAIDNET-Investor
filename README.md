## Overview

BAIDNET is an integrated financial technology and blockchain infrastructure platform developed within the BlackWall-Interconnected Co. ecosystem.

This repository serves as the public-facing information portal for BAIDNET's commercialization and market-entry initiative.

The purpose of this portal is to provide prospective funders, strategic partners, financial institutions, legal and regulatory advisors, and other authorized stakeholders with an organized point of reference for reviewing BAIDNET's platform, implementation status, readiness position, and remaining commercialization requirements.

---

## Current Platform Position

BAIDNET has progressed beyond the core platform-development stage.

The BAIDNET FinTech Extension has completed its defined internal engineering scope across Workstreams A through G, including:

- Existing blockchain-to-fiat capability mapping
- Canonical fiat transaction lifecycle
- In-house financial API extension
- Transaction identity and event correlation
- Audit-trail reconstruction
- Reconciliation and exception-management controls
- Sponsor-bank integration boundaries
- Failure, reversal, refund, and dispute workflows
- Monitoring and observability
- End-to-end scenario validation
- Definition-of-done verification

The implementation preserves a unified architecture consisting of one platform and common transaction model with multiple settlement paths, while maintaining explicit boundaries between BAIDNET-controlled functions and sponsor-bank-controlled functions.

## Commercialization & Market Entry Initiative

BAIDNET is seeking commercialization and market-entry capital to close the external requirements separating the completed internal platform from regulated commercial deployment.

The initiative is focused on five principal workstreams:

1. **Regulatory Classification & Financial Services Counsel**
   - Independent legal analysis
   - Asset and BDC classification
   - Securities considerations
   - Stored-value and prepaid-access considerations
   - Money-services and applicable financial-regulatory analysis
   - Contractual and regulatory positioning

2. **Compliance & Regulatory Advisory**
   - External compliance review
   - Policy and control validation
   - Regulatory-readiness documentation
   - Remediation of externally identified requirements

3. **Sponsor-Bank Engagement & Due Diligence**
   - Sponsor-bank identification and engagement
   - Due-diligence response
   - Responsibility and control mapping
   - Partner-specific requirements
   - Contractual allocation
   - Production-parity validation

4. **Production Enablement & Independent Validation**
   - Partner-specific configuration
   - Production credentials
   - Connectivity requirements
   - Settlement integration
   - Independent validation and readiness testing

5. **Documentation, Attestations & Deliverables**
   - Legal and regulatory deliverables
   - Sponsor-bank evidence packages
   - Production-readiness attestations
   - Integration documentation
   - Commercialization-readiness reporting

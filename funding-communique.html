Funding Pathway Communiqué · September 2026

One funding pathway.
Three accountable stages.

BAIDNET’s capital pathway progresses sequentially from grant-supported pilot establishment, through immediate controlled-deployment and validation capital, to later commercial gap closure and market entry. Each stage has a distinct purpose, evidence threshold, and funding audience.

Evidence boundary: BAIDNET is not represented as operating in commercial production, holding sponsor-bank authorization, possessing regulatory approval, or having completed independent security certification.

Stage 1: Grant-supported pilot establishment

Mission-aligned grants, philanthropic support, and programmatic funding can establish the environment and implementation capacity required to move beyond founder-supported development.

Current grant purpose

Fundable implementation activities

  • Controlled-pilot infrastructure and operational resilience.
  • Participant education, onboarding, access, and support.
  • Merchant recruitment, activation, training, and tools.
  • Participant-protection processes, compliance preparation, and policy refinement.
  • Community partnerships, independent review, and measurable reporting.
Grant boundary

Responsible positioning

  • Grant funding does not purchase equity or promise financial return.
  • Each request must follow the opportunity’s eligibility and allowable-cost rules.
  • Matching requirements, legal-entity eligibility, and committed deliverables must be confirmed before application.
  • Targets must remain distinguished from completed adoption or commercial outcomes.

Stage 2: Immediate controlled deployment

The technology foundation exists. The operating environment does not—yet.

The present constraint is not whether the platform can be packaged for deployment. It is funding the infrastructure and independent work required to operate it credibly under controlled pilot conditions.

Verified internally

Evidence already established

  • Application, payment, wallet, claims, refund, and transaction-management workflows documented in code and pilot evidence.
  • Fiat lifecycle, versioned financial primitives, audit reconstruction, reconciliation, and sponsor-bank boundary engineering documented within the stated internal scope.
  • A 21-container deployment image suite built successfully through GitHub Actions and published to a private container registry.
  • Monitoring, database, cache, blockchain RPC, validator, backend, payment, and frontend components packaged for deployment.
Funding dependent

Controlled-pilot requirements

  • Secure, continuously available remote container host and managed networking.
  • Production-grade secrets, durable storage, backups, controlled access, and disaster recovery.
  • Independent vulnerability assessment, penetration testing, remediation, and operational validation.
  • Legal classification and payments analysis, followed by sponsor-bank diligence and partner-specific integration.
  • Merchant and participant onboarding, support, measurement, and accountable reporting.

A staged path to market evidence

Capital converts technical reproducibility into an environment that institutions, pilot participants, legal reviewers, and prospective bank partners can evaluate responsibly.

01

Deploy

Stand up the complete suite in a secure remote environment with controlled administration and durable services.

02

Validate

Complete independent security work, legal scoping, operational testing, and documented remediation.

03

Implement

Activate a bounded merchant and participant pilot with defined safeguards, support, metrics, and reporting.

04

Measure

Evaluate availability, participation, transaction activity, claims, merchant engagement, and participant experience.

05

Engage

Use verified pilot evidence for sponsor-bank diligence, institutional partnerships, and external authorization work.

06

Scale responsibly

Advance only after legal, security, operational, and partner gates have been independently addressed.

BAIDNET’s immediate constraint is not whether the technology can be built. It is funding the secure infrastructure, independent validation, and implementation capacity required to operate that technology as a controlled pilot.

Grant, philanthropic, proof-of-concept, and mission-aligned investment pathways may each support different portions of this work.
